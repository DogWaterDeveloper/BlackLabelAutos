# Logo

Production lockups. Every file is two-tone or single-ink with its inks baked in, because an
asset served through `<img>` cannot inherit `currentColor`.

## Pick by ground

| Ground | File |
| --- | --- |
| Graphite, dark photography | `blacklabel-primary.svg`. Off-white BLACK, `gold-400` LABEL, gold plate |
| Paper, white, light photography | `blacklabel-primary-light.svg`. `graphite-950` BLACK, `gold-700` LABEL, deep gold plate |
| **A brand colour, a photograph, any ground at all** | `blacklabel-knockout.svg`. The crown is cut out of the plate as one even-odd path, so the ground shows through it |
| Busy imagery, one-colour print, etching | `blacklabel-mono-white.svg` / `blacklabel-mono-black.svg` |
| Gold foil, single-ink gold | `blacklabel-mono-gold.svg` |

## Which lockup

- `blacklabel-primary` is the default, at 132px wide or more.
- `blacklabel-knockout` is the same construction with the crown removed. Four inks: gold, deep gold
  (`-light`), white, graphite (`-black`).
- `blacklabel-wordmark` goes where the plate would fall below 20px, or where a plate already
  appears on the same surface.
- `blacklabel-stacked` fits square and near-square spaces: avatars, key fobs, packaging faces.
- `blacklabel-autos` / `-motorsport` / `-properties` are division lockups. `properties` is a
  stress test, not a real business; keep it as a reference for how any future descriptor sits.

## PNGs

`*-2400.png` are transparent-background exports 2400px wide; the stacked form is 2048px.
sRGB, no background, for slides, email and anywhere SVG is not accepted. They are exports, the SVGs are the masters.

## Rules

Clear space on every side is half the plate. Minimum 132px wide for the primary lockup, 96px
for the wordmark. Never recolour, outline, rotate, stretch or shadow any of these files, and
never place a solid two-tone lockup on a photograph or a brand colour. That is what the
knockout is for.
